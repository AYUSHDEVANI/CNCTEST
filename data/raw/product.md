# NovaSuite Product Overview

## What is NovaSuite

NovaSuite is a cloud-based SaaS platform that helps businesses manage customer support, internal documentation, team collaboration, and automated workflows from a single unified dashboard. It is designed for companies of all sizes, from early-stage startups to large enterprises with thousands of employees. NovaSuite eliminates the need for separate tools by combining everything into one platform.

## Core Philosophy

NovaSuite is built around the idea that support teams should spend less time switching between tools and more time resolving customer issues. The platform uses AI to automate repetitive work, so agents can focus on conversations that require human judgment. Every feature in NovaSuite is designed to be simple to set up but powerful enough for complex workflows.

## Help Desk and Ticketing

NovaSuite's Help Desk module allows support teams to receive, manage, and resolve customer tickets from a single unified inbox. Tickets can come in through email, live chat, WhatsApp Business, Facebook Messenger, Twitter direct messages, and web-based contact forms. Each ticket is automatically tagged, categorized, and prioritized using AI based on the content of the message.

Agents can set priority levels as Low, Medium, High, or Critical. SLA timers track how long a ticket has been open and send alerts before a breach occurs. Collision detection prevents two agents from working on the same ticket at the same time. Agents can also leave internal notes on tickets that are visible only to the team and not to the customer.

## Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base module allows teams to create and publish articles for customers or internal staff. Articles support rich text and Markdown formatting. The knowledge base supports nested categories, article versioning with rollback, full-text search, and multi-language publishing across 28 supported languages. Article-level analytics show how many times an article was viewed and whether customers found it helpful.

Access control allows articles to be set as public, internal-only, or restricted to specific roles. A help widget can be embedded in any website or web application so customers can search the knowledge base without leaving the page. When a customer opens a support ticket, NovaSuite automatically suggests relevant articles before the agent responds.

## Team Collaboration

NovaSuite includes a shared team inbox and internal messaging system. Team members can communicate through threaded messages, use at-mentions to notify specific colleagues, and share files up to 500 megabytes per upload. Status indicators show whether each team member is Online, Away, or on Do Not Disturb. Notifications from NovaSuite can be mirrored into Slack or Microsoft Teams so agents do not need to switch applications.

## Workflow Automation

The Workflow Builder lets teams automate repetitive tasks without writing code. Workflows are built using triggers, conditions, and actions. A trigger might be a ticket being created or an SLA being breached. Conditions allow branching logic such as checking if a ticket has a specific priority or tag. Actions can include assigning a ticket to an agent, sending an email, escalating to a manager, or calling an external webhook.

NovaSuite includes pre-built workflow templates for common scenarios such as auto-closing idle tickets after a set number of days, escalating unresolved critical tickets after two hours, and sending follow-up tasks when a customer leaves a low satisfaction rating. Each workflow keeps a version history and an execution log to help teams debug issues.

## Reports and Analytics

The Reports module gives managers visibility into team performance and customer satisfaction. Available reports include ticket volume by channel, agent, or time period, average first response time, average resolution time, SLA compliance rate, and customer satisfaction scores over time. Knowledge base reports show which articles are most viewed and which search queries returned no results.

A custom report builder allows teams to create their own views and export data as CSV or PDF files. Historical data is available for up to 12 months on the Growth plan and unlimited on Business and Enterprise plans.

## Security and Administration

NovaSuite supports Single Sign-On through SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0. Two-factor authentication can be enforced across all accounts. Role-based access control includes four default roles: Admin, Manager, Agent, and Viewer. IP whitelisting restricts access to known network addresses. All admin and agent actions are recorded in an audit log.

Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. NovaSuite is fully GDPR compliant and offers tools for data export, contact deletion, and configurable data retention policies. HIPAA compliance is available for Enterprise customers who sign a Business Associate Agreement.

## Platform Availability

NovaSuite is available as a web application at app.novasuite.io, a mobile app on iOS and Android, a desktop app for Windows and macOS, and browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox. The platform maintains a 99.95 percent monthly uptime SLA, with real-time status available at status.novasuite.io. Enterprise customers receive a 99.99 percent uptime SLA with dedicated infrastructure.

## Data Residency

Customer data is stored in one of three AWS regions depending on the selected plan. The default region is US East in Northern Virginia. European customers can choose EU Central in Frankfurt, Germany. APAC customers can choose Asia Pacific in Singapore. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom data residency agreements including private cloud deployments.
