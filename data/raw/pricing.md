# NovaSuite Pricing and Plans

## Overview

NovaSuite offers four pricing tiers: Starter, Growth, Business, and Enterprise. All plans can be billed monthly or annually. Annual billing provides a flat 20 percent discount across all plans. Prices listed are exclusive of applicable taxes such as VAT or GST, which may be added based on your billing country.

## Starter Plan

The Starter plan costs 29 dollars per month and supports up to three agent seats. It is designed for freelancers, small teams, and early-stage startups. The Starter plan includes the Help Desk module with email channel support only, a knowledge base with up to 50 articles, up to five automated workflows, and one gigabyte of file storage. Analytics are available for the last 30 days only.

The Starter plan does not include API access, SSO, two-factor authentication, custom domain for the knowledge base, or SLA management. Support is limited to community forums and documentation. The NovaSuite branding appears on the live chat widget and cannot be removed on this plan.

## Growth Plan

The Growth plan costs 79 dollars per month and includes up to ten agent seats. Additional agents beyond the included ten can be added for nine dollars per agent per month. This plan is designed for growing teams and small to mid-sized businesses.

The Growth plan includes all features from Starter, plus support for all channels including WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Twitter, and live chat. The knowledge base supports unlimited articles. Up to 25 automated workflows are available. SLA management and breach alerts are included. The NovaSuite branding can be removed from the chat widget. File storage is increased to 10 gigabytes. Analytics history extends to 12 months. Two-factor authentication is supported. Email support is available with a 24-hour response time. The knowledge base can be published on a custom domain. Read-only REST API access is included.

## Business Plan

The Business plan costs 199 dollars per month and includes up to 30 agent seats. Additional agents beyond 30 can be added for seven dollars per agent per month. This plan is designed for mid-market companies with high support volume.

The Business plan includes everything in Growth, plus unlimited automated workflows, AI-assisted ticket routing and automatic response suggestions, AI-powered article suggestions when tickets are opened, a custom report builder with unlimited analytics history, and 100 gigabytes of file storage. Full read and write REST API access is available. SSO via SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0 is supported. Custom roles can be defined within the role-based access control system. Audit logs are retained for 90 days. Outbound webhooks are available. Slack and Microsoft Teams integrations are included. Priority email and live chat support is provided with a four-hour response time.

## Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan is priced through a custom quote based on seat count, usage, and required features. It requires an annual contract with a minimum term of 12 months. This plan is designed for large organizations, regulated industries, and companies with multi-region deployment needs.

The Enterprise plan includes everything in Business, plus unlimited agent seats, a dedicated account manager, custom SLA agreements, an option for on-premise deployment using Docker, custom data residency in any supported region or private cloud, dedicated infrastructure with no resource sharing, a 99.99 percent uptime SLA, 24/7 phone and email support with under one hour response for critical incidents, custom integrations and API development support, advanced security features including SIEM integration and data loss prevention policies, GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2 Type II compliance certifications, employee onboarding and training sessions, and unlimited audit log retention. White-labeling with custom branding is fully supported on this plan.

To request an Enterprise quote, contact sales@novasuite.io or visit novasuite.io/enterprise.

## Annual Billing Savings

Choosing annual billing saves 20 percent compared to monthly billing. The Starter plan drops from 29 dollars per month to approximately 23.20 dollars per month when billed annually, saving around 69 dollars per year. The Growth plan drops from 79 dollars to approximately 63.20 dollars per month, saving around 189 dollars per year. The Business plan drops from 199 dollars to approximately 159.20 dollars per month, saving around 478 dollars per year.

## Add-Ons

Several optional add-ons are available on the Growth plan and above. Extra storage can be added in 100 gigabyte increments for 15 dollars per month. An additional workflow pack adding 25 more workflows costs 19 dollars per month. An advanced AI responses feature powered by GPT-4 is available for 49 dollars per month. Custom SSO configuration assistance is available as a one-time service for 299 dollars. Priority onboarding is available as a one-time service for 499 dollars.

## Free Trial

NovaSuite offers a 14-day free trial on the Growth and Business plans. No credit card is required to start a trial. Full feature access is available during the trial period. All data created during the trial is preserved when you upgrade to a paid plan. If you do not upgrade within 14 days, your account is automatically placed in read-only mode and no data is deleted.

## Refund Policy

Monthly plan customers cannot receive a refund for the current billing period but can cancel at any time before the next cycle begins. Annual plan customers can request a full refund within the first 30 days of the annual term. After 30 days, no refunds are issued but the account remains active until the end of the annual term. Enterprise contract refund terms are defined in the signed agreement.

## Payment Methods

NovaSuite accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal for all plans. Enterprise customers may also pay via wire transfer or invoice with NET-30 payment terms. All card transactions are processed securely through Stripe. NovaSuite does not store card details directly.

## Upgrading and Downgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately and billing is prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. When you downgrade, your data is not immediately deleted. A 30-day grace period is provided to export or adjust your data before any limits are enforced.
